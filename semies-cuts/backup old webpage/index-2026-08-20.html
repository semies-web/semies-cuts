QUESTIONS?

Everything you need to know before booking

How long does a haircut take? ▼

Classic cuts take 30-45 minutes. Premium styles and detailed work take 45-60 minutes. Royal Treatment can go up to 2 hours depending on what you choose. We never rush—quality comes first.

Do you do walk-ins or is it appointment only? ▼

We operate by appointment to ensure you get quality time and no wait. Book in-shop or home service directly through WhatsApp or email. This keeps the experience smooth for you and allows us to give every client full attention.

What's your cancellation policy? ▼

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment for a full refund of your deposit. Less notice and the deposit stays, but we'll happily book you another time. Life happens—just give us a heads-up.

How does home service work? ▼

After you book, we'll confirm the exact address and time. I bring all tools and equipment—you just provide a comfortable space (chair, good lighting, mirror). No setup needed on your end. It's the same quality cut, just at your convenience.

What's your aftercare advice? ▼

Keep your fade clean by rinsing with cold water daily. If you got beard work, use beard oil 2-3 times a week. Stay out of direct sunlight right after (especially if you're new to a style). We'll give you a take-home care sheet with every cut if requested.

Can I show you a reference photo? ▼

Absolutely. Bring reference photos—the clearer, the better. During your consultation, we'll talk through exactly what you want, what suits your hair type, and what's realistic for your routine. We listen before we cut.
